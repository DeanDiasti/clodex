# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in the Clodex community a harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or
ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and specific.
- Assume good intent while addressing the substance of disagreements.
- Give and accept actionable feedback gracefully.
- Protect private information, especially credentials and account data.
- Focus on what is best for users and the community.

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, threats, sexualized attention,
and publishing another person's private information are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately through the repository owner's GitHub
profile. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or
permanently restrict participation when behavior is inappropriate, harmful, or
threatening.

This policy applies in all project spaces and when representing the project in
public spaces.
